# The three strategies

**Read this file before answering anything about strategy. Do not reconstruct it
from memory.** Confirmed by Nik 2026-08-20.

Three books run side by side. They are not variations of one idea. Two of them
begin by selling a put, which is where they get confused with each other, and
that confusion has now cost several sessions.

---

## Income sleeve

Own a block of shares in a name near its 52-week low with conviction. Every week
sell one ATM call and one ATM put per 100 shares. Hold an ATM 4-month put
underneath as the floor.

The names do not change week to week. The floor is paid for and the conviction
is why the name is there.

**Never a standalone put.** Entering a name means four legs at once: buy the
shares, sell the call, sell the put, buy the floor. "Write N puts on X" is not a
sentence this strategy can produce.

---

## TLT

No block. No conviction.

Sell puts on the second red day of a slide, nearest Friday, nearest strike, flat
size. Assignment is how you buy it.

**The put IS the trade.** There is no block underneath and nothing to protect.

---

## NVDA

Puts on Fridays only. An income wheel, not accumulation.

---

## What separates them

|  | income sleeve | TLT | NVDA |
|---|---|---|---|
| owns shares first | yes | no | no |
| the put is | one of four legs | the whole trade | the trade |
| conviction in the name | yes, that is why it is there | none | n/a |
| a floor underneath | yes, ATM 4-month | no | see NVDA docs |
| names change weekly | **no** | n/a | n/a |

## The two mistakes to check yourself against

**Do not apply a new-money test to a held block.** An edge floor decides where
NEW capital goes. A block already owned, with a floor already bought and
decaying, is written every week. Skipping it leaves the floor bleeding with
nothing paying for it, and the spec is explicit that premium funds the floor.

**Do not treat the sleeve as weekly.** Nik: *"I cant buy Nike one week and not
buy next week, this is not a weekly game plan, the puts bought will cost me. Plus
being 52 week low or near that means my conviction is high, to hold them not toss
them away each week or day."*
