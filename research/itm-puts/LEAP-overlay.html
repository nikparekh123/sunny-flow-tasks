Know before you trade

  • Delta. Every combination starts delta positive. After a big fall the hedged versions turn short for a while: on 34% of days on the no-view paths, against 15% today. The only rule that prevents it (sell calls only while delta stays above +20%) gives up half the crash protection (+$103K instead of +$208K).
  • TSLA, SPCX and APP. Their weekly options are priced below how much they move (0.90 to 0.99), so selling calls on them loses money while that lasts; switch to "Stays as today" to see it. Anything bought on them looks cheap for the same reason. SPCX has only 4 months of trading; the rest is TSLA's moves scaled to SPCX.
  • LULU and NFLX gain less. Their LEAP strikes sit near the price, so the LEAP already acts like a put below the strike.
  • INTU is the most expensive to hedge. Its puts are priced above how much it has moved.
  • Margin. Your account is on portfolio margin, which sizes margin from the worst loss over roughly a 15% move either way. On your four names that worst loss falls from $36.6K (a drop) to $23.3K (now a rise, because the puts lose value in a rally): about a third less margin. Across all ten, $113K to $77K.
  • Sizes. NKE, LULU, BABA and NFLX use your real LEAPs. ADBE, INTU, SPCX, TSLA, PYPL and APP are example blocks of about $150K of stock, Jan 2028 LEAPs near 0.80 delta (SPCX 0.85). The tables use exact ratios; the trade sheet rounds them.

How the test works

A forward test, not a backtest: these names fell 20 to 30% in two years, so history would flatter any put. Each path starts at today's price and runs 12 months. Daily moves are drawn in 5-day blocks from the stock's own last 3 years with the biggest days trimmed, plus an earnings jump on each report date, then bent to hit the scenario. The first puts and calls are priced at today's real quotes; later ones at today's implied volatility moved with the path. Weekly options are priced from the last 20 days' moves with report days left out (a report's own move is added only when it falls inside the week), times a richness: today's measured value, either fading to normal (1.05) within about two months or held all year. LEAPs are valued with Black-Scholes. Fills cost a quarter of the spread on long options and 2% plus $0.65 a contract on weekly ones.
