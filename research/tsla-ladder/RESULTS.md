# Three-expiry ladder: calls bought, every version

Model research, not advice. 1,000 shares; 10 calls sold on each of the next three expiries (TSLA Mon/Wed/Fri, SPY/QQQ/IWM every trading day), every third sale at the money, the others 2/3/5% out; calls bought re-sized on each expiry day to keep 'could be' (the lower net delta after an instant 5% move) between 0 and +300. Real Theta end-of-day quotes. Totals in $K: at mid, and at bid/ask (sell at the bid, buy at the ask).
